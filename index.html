How it works

Four hours, roughly

Nothing here is rushed and nothing is skipped. If a step throws up a problem, you hear about it before we carry on.

What is a remap? →
00:00

Health check first

Fault codes read and the engine looked over before anything is touched. A remap leans on an existing fault rather than hiding it, so if something needs sorting you'll know now — not after.

00:20

Your original file is saved

The factory software is read off and archived against your registration. That's your guarantee the car can go back to standard whenever you want it to.

01:30

The new file is written

Built around your engine, its mileage and its condition, with the manufacturer's safety margins left where they are. Then loaded onto the car.

03:30

Road tested and logged again

Driven and measured a second time. If the numbers coming back off the car disagree with the file, the file changes — not the other way round.

Straight answers

Questions we get asked most

If yours isn't here, ring and ask. You'll get a straight answer even when it's not the one you were hoping for.

Is it legal?
Yes. Remapping is legal in the UK. You must declare it to your insurer and we give you the details in writing to pass on. What isn't legal is removing emissions equipment from a road vehicle, which is why we don't do it.
Will it affect my insurance?
It has to be declared, like any modification. Most insurers handle it without much fuss; an undeclared remap is the fastest way to have a claim refused.
What about my warranty?
If the vehicle is still under manufacturer warranty, assume a remap affects it. We'll talk it through honestly before you book rather than after, and the original file is archived so it can go back before a dealer visit.
Can it be undone?
Yes. Your factory file is read off and stored against your registration before anything is written, so the vehicle can be returned to exactly how it left the factory.
Will it use more fuel?
Driven the same way, usually slightly less, because there's more torque low down. Driven to enjoy the extra power, obviously more.
Does anything get replaced?
Not on a standard remap. No parts come off and nothing is cut. The car looks identical when you collect it.
