Stages

"Stage" isn't a power figure. It's a list of parts.

We match the file to the hardware that's actually on the car, never the other way round.

S1

Stage 1 — standard car

Nothing changed under the bonnet. Everything gained comes from calibration within what the standard turbo, injectors, clutch and cooling will support. This is the right answer for the large majority of cars.

S2

Stage 2 — supporting mods

Intake, exhaust or intercooler already fitted. The file is written around what's genuinely on the vehicle, so bring the spec list when you book.

BSP

Bespoke

Track use, heavy towing, a specific rev range, or a car modified beyond the usual. Priced after we've seen it and logged it, not before.

Drivability

A car that's quicker but worse to drive is a failed job

Power that arrives in a lump is easy to write and unpleasant to live with. Getting a curve that pulls cleanly from low down and keeps pulling takes far longer, and it's the part you'll notice every single day.

Pedal response, part-throttle behaviour, how it holds a gear on a hill, whether it's still smooth pulling out of a junction cold. None of it shows on a dyno printout. All of it shows on the drive home.
